Movmi: AI Powered 3D Motion Capture and Animation Creator

Role: Co-founder & Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Period: 2020 - 2024

Recognition: Accepted by NVIDIA Inception & Microsoft Startups Program

Total Users: 12,000+

Website: movmi.co

About

Movmi is an AI-powered 3D motion capture solution that allows content creators, animators, and game developers to generate high-quality 3D animations from 2D video input without expensive equipment or markers.

Key Achievements

  • Accepted into NVIDIA Inception Program for AI startups
  • Selected for Microsoft Startups Program
  • Grew user base to 12,000+ creators and developers
  • Developed proprietary computer vision algorithms for markerless motion capture
  • Built real-time 3D pose estimation and animation pipeline
  • Created web-based platform for accessible 3D animation creation

Technologies Used

  • Computer Vision and Deep Learning
  • 3D Pose Estimation (MediaPipe, OpenPose)
  • Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
  • 3D Graphics and Animation (Three.js, Blender)
  • NVIDIA GPU acceleration for real-time processing
  • WebGL for browser-based 3D rendering

Impact

Movmi democratized 3D motion capture technology, making it accessible to independent creators, small studios, and educators who previously couldn't afford expensive motion capture equipment. The platform has been used for game development, animation production, fitness applications, and educational content.

Entrepreneurial Philosophy

My approach to building startups combines deep technical expertise with a focus on solving real problems for users. I believe in:

  • User-Centric Innovation: Building products that solve genuine pain points in research and creative workflows
  • Technical Excellence: Leveraging cutting-edge AI and ML techniques to deliver superior performance
  • Scalability: Designing systems that can grow from hundreds to thousands of users
  • Accessibility: Making advanced technology accessible to broader audiences
  • Continuous Learning: Staying at the forefront of AI research to improve products

Lessons Learned

  • Importance of product-market fit and early user feedback
  • Balancing technical innovation with business viability
  • Building and managing cross-border remote teams
  • Navigating startup accelerators and funding ecosystems
  • Scaling infrastructure and managing technical debt
  • Importance of partnerships with major tech companies (NVIDIA, Microsoft)
